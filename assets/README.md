## Sahyadri opening photograph

`sahyadri-torna-rajgad.webp` is a resized WebP derivative of [Way to Rajgad from Torna.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Way_to_Rajgad_from_Torna.jpg), photographed by Sopan Patil in 2017. Source and derivative: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The opening crops the frame to the viewport and adds a lighting overlay. This is a modern landscape photograph.
