## Mandatory citation standard
Each final chapter assertion requiring evidence must record: claim ID, original-language source, edition/editor, volume, page or document identifier, date, quotation/transcription where permitted, interpretation, conflicting testimony, verification status, and reviewer note.

Statuses:
- **lead**: catalog or bibliography located
- **located**: actual edition or scan obtained
- **checked**: specific passage read in that edition
- **corroborated**: independently compared against another relevant source
- **disputed**: conflicting accounts or interpretation
- **unverified**: no adequate support yet

No chapter should be called historically authenticated merely because this register lists a book. Avoid invented manuscript transcripts, exact dialogue, unsupported numerical claims and unsourced dates.
